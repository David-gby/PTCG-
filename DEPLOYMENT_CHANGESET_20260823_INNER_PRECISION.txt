Important production rule:
- Strong 58x83 mm span locking is enabled only when outer geometry is independently trusted.
- Ordinary historical photos must remain on the conservative route. On 95 locked real-photo
  samples, the conservative route achieved 1.382 px mean four-edge MAE and 3.177 px P95;
  forcibly treating the old photos as exact crops regressed to 2.158 px and 4.094 px.
- Do not add a global force-trusted switch during deployment.

Server update (existing installation):
1. Back up /var/lib/cardscope/platform_workspace.
2. Upload the zip without extracting it into the live release directory.
3. Run:
   sudo bash /opt/cardscope/current/deployment/server/update_release.sh <zip-path> <https://public-domain>
4. Confirm /api/platform/v1/health and that the returned model/pipeline version is
   ptcg_outer_inner_pipeline_20260823_inner_precision_v2.

Never overwrite or delete:
- /var/lib/cardscope/platform_workspace
- enterprise accounts, inspections, feedback, annotations and training-pool data
- /etc/cardscope/cardscope.env, domain/TLS configuration or access credentials
